Quick Capture

Quick Capture can be installed as a small, separate Unbound app. It opens directly into the capture screen and saves to your Notes & Ideas Inbox. Your existing Unbound app stays installed as it is.

Set up the Side button

  1. Open this page in Chrome on your phone: https://unbound-development.pages.dev/quick-capture.html.
  2. Open Chrome’s ⋮ menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen. The app should be named Unbound Quick Capture.
  3. After it appears as its own app on your phone, open Settings → Advanced features → Side button → Double press → Open app.
  4. Select Unbound Quick Capture. Double-press the Side button to open Dictate, Camera and Type.

If Chrome reports that this app is already installed, cancel without replacing Unbound. The Quick Capture install uses its own app identity; refresh this page in Chrome and check the ⋮ menu again. Samsung may label the setting Side key. Your original Camera shortcut can be restored from the same Side button settings.
