Quick Capture

Quick Capture is a feature inside Unbound. It saves dictated notes, photos and typed thoughts to the Notes & Ideas Inbox. Bookmarking this help page saves these instructions; it does not create a button shortcut.

Open Quick Capture

  1. In Unbound, open Notes & Ideas and tap Quick Add, or use the Quick Capture shortcut from the Unbound app icon if your phone shows it.
  2. To use the Side button, open Settings → Advanced features → Side button → Double press → Open app and choose Unbound.
  3. If Samsung shows a settings gear beside Unbound, check whether it offers the Quick Capture shortcut. If it does, select it.

Samsung’s Side button normally launches an app. If its settings do not offer Quick Capture as an Unbound shortcut, the button will open Unbound at its regular screen; a browser bookmark cannot change that. You can still open the capture screen from the Unbound app icon shortcut, when available, or from Notes & Ideas → Quick Add. Dictation starts when you tap Start dictation.
