Quick Capture

If Chrome says Unbound is already installed, do not install it again or replace the existing app. Quick Capture is a shortcut inside Unbound, not a second app.

Open Quick Capture from Unbound

  1. Close this guide and open Unbound, or tap the button below.
  2. For a home-screen shortcut, long-press the Unbound app icon and choose Quick Capture if it appears.
  3. For the Side button, open Settings → Advanced features → Side button → Double press → Open app and choose Unbound.
  4. If a settings gear appears beside Unbound, check whether it offers Quick Capture.

The Side button may only open Unbound at its normal planner screen. Samsung and Chrome do not expose a guaranteed way for this PWA to pass the Quick Capture shortcut to the button. Check the gear option on your phone; if Quick Capture is not offered, keep Camera assigned and use the home-screen shortcut or browser Share → Unbound. You can restore the Camera assignment in the same Side button settings.
