# Country and territory data

The 249 ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 entries used by Unbound are enriched from [mledoze/countries](https://github.com/mledoze/countries), based on ISO 3166 data, licensed under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). See [ODbL-1.0.txt](./ODbL-1.0.txt).

Continent assignment uses the dataset’s UN geoscheme `region` and `subregion`: Africa, Asia, Europe, Oceania and Antarctica map directly; `Americas` is split by subregion into South America and North America (Caribbean, Central America and Northern America). Thus transcontinental states follow the dataset’s single primary region. Territories follow their assigned subregion. The dataset’s `altSpellings` and common English/French/German/Spanish/Portuguese/Italian/Dutch translations are searchable aliases. Entries are countries and territories; the 249-entry scope is the app’s existing list, not a claim that every entry is a sovereign state.
